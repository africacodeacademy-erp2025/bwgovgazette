INDUSTRY: CONSTRUCTION
======================

Division 41: Construction of Buildings

  Class 410: Construction of Buildings
    Group 4100: Construction of Buildings
Description:
  construction of all types of residential buildings; construction of all types of non-residential
  building; assembly and erection of prefabricated constructions on the site; remodelling or
  renovating existing residential structures 71
Division 42: Civil Engineering

  Class 421: Construction of Roads and Railways
    Group 4210: Construction of Roads and Railways
Description:
  construction of motorways, streets, roads, other vehicular and pedestrian ways; surface work on
  streets, roads, highways, bridges or tunnels; construction of bridges, including those for
  elevated highways; construction of tunnels; construction of railways and subways; construction of
  airfield runways.
  Class 422: Construction of Utility Projects (e.g Dams, Reservoirs, Pipelines)
    Group 4220: Construction of Utility Projects (e.g Dams, Reservoirs, Pipelines)
Description:
  construction of civil engineering constructions for : (long-distance pipelines, communication and
  power lines , urban pipelines, urban communication and power lines; ancillary urban works, water
  main and line construction, irrigation systems, reservoirs); construction of: (sewer systems,
  including repair, sewage disposal plants, pumping stations, power plants); water well drilling.
  Class 429: Construction of Other Civil Engineering Projects
    Group 4290: Construction of Other Civil Engineering Projects
Description:
  construction of industrial facilities, except buildings (refineries, chemical plants); construction
  of (waterways, harbour and river works, pleasure ports, locks), dams and dykes; dredging of
  waterways; construction work, other than buildings, such as (outdoor sports facilities); land
  subdivision with land improvement (e.g. adding of roads, utility infrastructure).
Division 43: Specialised Construction Activities

  Class 431: Demolition and Site Preparation
    Group 4311: Demolition
Description:
  demolition or wrecking of buildings and other structures
    Group 4312: Site Preparation
Description:
  includes the preparation of sites for subsequent construction activities; clearing of building
  sites; earth moving: excavation, landfill, levelling and grading of construction sites, trench
  digging, rock removal, blasting; drilling, boring and core sampling for construction, geophysical,
  geological or similar purposes; site preparation for mining; building site drainage; drainage of
  agricultural or forestry land.
  Class 432: Electrical, Plumbing and Other Construction Installation Activities
    Group 4321: Electrical Installation
Description:
  installation of electrical wiring and fittings, telecommunications wiring, computer network and
  cable television wiring, including fibre optic, satellite dishes, lighting systems, fire alarms,
  burglar alarm systems, street lighting and electrical signals, airport runway lighting.
    Group 4322: Plumbing, Heat and Air Conditioning Installation
Description:
  includes the installation of plumbing, heating and air-conditioning systems, including additions,
  alterations, maintenance and repair; installation in buildings or other construction projects of
  (heating systems (electric, gas and oil), furnaces, cooling towers, non-electric solar energy
  collectors, plumbing and sanitary equipment, ventilation, refrigeration or air-conditioning
  equipment and ducts, gas fittings, steam piping, fire sprinkler systems, lawn sprinkler systems,
  duct work installation. 72
    Group 4329: Other Construction Installation Activities
Description:
  includes the installation of equipment other than electrical, plumbing, heating and air-conditioning
  systems or industrial machinery in buildings and civil engineering structures, including
  maintenance and repair. This Includes installation in buildings or other construction projects of
  elevators, escalators, automated and revolving doors, lightning conductors, vacuum cleaning
  systems, thermal, sound or vibration insulation.
  Class 433: Building Completion and Finishing
    Group 4330: Building Completion and Finishing
Description:
  application in buildings or other construction projects of interior and exterior plaster or stucco,
  including related lathing materials; installation of doors (except automated and revolving),
  windows, door and window frames, of wood or other materials; installation of fitted kitchens,
  staircases, shop fittings; installation of furniture; interior completion such as ceilings, wooden
  wall coverings, movable partitions; laying, tiling, hanging or fitting in buildings or other
  construction projects ; interior and exterior painting of buildings; painting of civil engineering
  structures; installation of glass, mirrors; cleaning of new buildings after construction; other
  building completion work ; interior installation of shops, mobile homes, boats.
  Class 439: Other Specialised Construction Activities
    Group 4390: Other Specialised Construction Activities
Description:
  construction activities specializing in one aspect common to different kind of structures, requiring
  specialized skill or equipment (construction of foundations, including pile driving, damp proofing
  and water proofing works, de-humidification of buildings, shaft sinking, erection of non-self-
  manufactured steel elements, steel bending, bricklaying and stone setting, roof covering for
  residential buildings, scaffolds and work platform erecting and dismantling, excluding renting of
  scaffolds and work platforms, erection of chimneys and industrial ovens, work with specialist
  access requirements necessitating climbing skills and the use of related equipment, e.g. working
  at height on tall structures), subsurface work; construction of outdoor swimming pools; steam
  cleaning, sand blasting and similar activities for building exteriors; renting of cranes with
  operator.
