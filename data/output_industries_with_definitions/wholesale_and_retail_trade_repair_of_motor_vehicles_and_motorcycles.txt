INDUSTRY: WHOLESALE AND RETAIL TRADE; REPAIR OF MOTOR VEHICLES AND MOTORCYCLES
==============================================================================

Division 45: Wholesale and Retail Trade and Repair of Motor Vehicles and Motor Cycles

  Class 451: Sale of Motor Vehicles
    Group 4510: Sale of Motor Vehicles
Description:
  wholesale and retail sale of new and used vehicles (passenger motor vehicles, including specialized
  passenger motor vehicles such as ambulances and minibuses, lorries, trailers and semi-trailers,
  camping vehicles such as caravans and motor homes); wholesale and retail sale of off-road motor
  vehicles; wholesale and retail sale by commission agents; car auctions. 73
  Class 452: Maintenance and Repair of Motor Vehicles
    Group 4520: Maintenance and Repair of Motor Vehicles
Description:
  maintenance and repair of motor vehicles; tyre and tube repair, fitting or replacement; anti- rust
  treatment installation of parts and accessories not as part of the manufacturing process.
  Class 453: Sale of Motor Vehicle Parts and Accessories
    Group 4530: Sale of Motor Vehicle Parts and Accessories
Description:
  wholesale and retail sale of all kinds of parts, components, supplies, tools and accessories for
  motor vehicles, such as: rubber tires and inner tubes for tires, spark plugs, batteries, lighting
  equipment and electrical parts.
  Class 454: Sale, Maintenance and Repair of Motor Cycles and Related Parts and Accessories
    Group 4540: Sale, Maintenance and Repair of Motor Cycles and Related Parts and Accessories
Description:
  and Accessories: wholesale and retail sale of motorcycles, including mopeds; wholesale and retail
  sale of parts and accessories for motorcycles; maintenance and repair of motorcycles.
