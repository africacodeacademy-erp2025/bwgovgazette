INDUSTRY: ACTIVITIES OF EXTRATERRITORIAL ORGANIZATIONS AND BODIES
=================================================================

Division 99: Activities of Extraterritorial Organizations and Bodies

  Class 990: Activities of Extraterritorial Organizations and Bodies
    Group 9900: Activities of Extraterritorial Organizations and Bodies
Description:
  activities of international organizations such as the United Nations and the specialized agencies of
  the United Nations system, regional bodies etc., the International Monetary Fund, the World Bank,
  the World Customs Organization, the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development, the
  Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries, the European Communities, the European Free Trade
  Association; activities of diplomatic and consular missions when being determined by the country
  of their location rather than by the country they represent. 112
